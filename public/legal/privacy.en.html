2. Summary

  • We only process data that is needed to run the platform and the features you use.
  • We use no tracking, no analytics tools and no advertising, and we do not sell data.
  • Some features (Google sign-in, maps, place search, video chat) involve external services – details below.

3. Hosting and server log files

The platform runs on servers of a hosting provider. When you access it, the server processes technically necessary data: IP address, date and time, requested address, browser and operating system. This is required for delivery, security and troubleshooting (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Log files are only kept as long as necessary for these purposes.

4. User account

When you register, we process your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash) and your display name. Optionally, you can add a profile picture, a cover image and further profile details. To protect your account, we store signed-in devices and sessions. The legal basis is the performance of the contract of use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Sign-in with Google

If you sign in with Google, the app redirects you to Google (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Google then sends us your name, email address, Google account ID and – if available – your profile picture. Data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA; Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. The legal basis is your consent by choosing this sign-in method (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

5. Content you create

We store the content you publish or create: timeline posts, images, comments and reactions, forum posts, chat messages, resumes, feedback tickets and friend requests. You decide who can see this content via the respective visibility settings. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. To combat abuse, we may review reported content (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Location

We only use your device location if you actively share it – for example to add a place to a post. Your browser or operating system asks for permission first; you can withdraw it at any time. To turn coordinates into an address (and vice versa), our server sends the coordinates or search term to Nominatim of the OpenStreetMap Foundation (St John’s Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom). The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). The EU Commission has issued an adequacy decision for the United Kingdom.

7. Maps

For map views, the app loads map tiles from CARTO (carto.com) with data from OpenStreetMap. Your IP address is transmitted to CARTO, as the tiles cannot be delivered otherwise. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working map display (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Video chat, meetings and file transfer

Video, audio and transferred files go directly between the devices involved (peer-to-peer via WebRTC) and are not stored on our servers. Our server only brokers the connection. For technical reasons, the participants learn each other's device IP address. To establish connections, the app also uses public STUN servers from Google, which receive your IP address for this purpose. Camera and microphone are only used after you allow them in your browser or operating system. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

The random video chat is only available from age 18; we store your confirmation locally on your device.

9. Resume editor

When generating a resume PDF, the app loads font files via the jsDelivr content delivery network. Your IP address is transmitted to the CDN operator (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – consistent, correct rendering of the PDF).

10. Emails

We send you emails that are necessary for your account – e.g. to confirm your address, reset your password or report status changes of your tickets (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We do not send advertising newsletters.

11. Storage on your device

The app stores technically necessary information in your browser's local storage (local storage, session storage) and in one cookie: sign-in state and device ID, language, appearance (light/dark), recent search terms, menu and notice settings and the sidebar state. This storage is strictly necessary for the features you request to work (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). You can delete this data in your browser at any time; you will then be signed out.

12. Recipients

Your data is only received by the service providers named above and – where you share content – by the users you make it visible to. We only disclose data to authorities where we are legally obliged to do so.

13. Retention

We store account data and content for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account (account settings → “Delete account”), your account and the associated data are deleted unless statutory retention obligations apply. Content that others have already received (e.g. chat messages) may remain with them.

14. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)). Simply contact us at the address above.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the member state of your residence.

15. Changes

We update this privacy policy when the app or the law changes. The version published here applies.
